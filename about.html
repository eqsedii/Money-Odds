HOW IT WORKS

Real data. Real statistics. No false promises.

Money Odds exists because most "prediction" sites are guesswork dressed up with confident language. Ours isn't — here's exactly how it works.

Our process

Every prediction on this site is generated the same automated way, every time — no picks are hand-selected or adjusted after the fact.

1
Pull real fixture and results data
We fetch upcoming matches and recently completed results directly from football-data.org (12 major competitions) and balldontlie.io (NBA) — the same kind of sources sports journalists and analysts use.
2
Run a statistical model, not a guess
Football predictions use a Poisson goal-expectancy model built from each team's actual home/away scoring and conceding record this season. Basketball uses a scoring-differential model from recent games. Neither is influenced by opinion.
3
Publish every market's probability
We don't just show you the pick — every match page shows the full breakdown across every market we modeled, so you can see the actual math, not just our headline.
4
Grade every result, automatically
Once a match finishes, our system checks the real final score and marks the pick won or lost — no manual editing, no cherry-picking what gets shown on the Track Record page.

What we won't do

  • We will never claim a guaranteed win. No prediction service — ours included — can guarantee a sports outcome. Anyone who tells you otherwise is being dishonest with you.
  • We won't hide our losses. Our Track Record page shows every graded prediction, win or lose, generated the same automated way.
  • We won't fabricate statistics. Every number on this site — confidence percentages, expected goals, head-to-head records, recent form — is computed from real data, not invented for effect.
